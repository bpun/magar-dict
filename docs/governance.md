# Magar Dictionary: Content Governance

**Version:** 1.0 (Day 2 baseline)
**Applies to:** all words, forms, examples, audio, and AI output.

---

## 1. Principles

1. **The community owns the language.** The project is a steward of the
   data, not its owner. Contributors keep the right to be credited,
   corrected, or removed.
2. **Nothing public is unverified.** Guests and learners see only content
   a native speaker of that variety has approved.
3. **No variety speaks for another.** A verifier approves only the
   variety they belong to.
4. **AI assists; people decide.** AI output never reaches the public
   without human approval.
5. **Every change is traceable.** Who changed what, when, and why is
   recorded and cannot be edited.

## 2. Roles

| Role | Who | Can do | Cannot do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Language lead | One named person | Owns scope, romanization schemes, and this document; breaks ties between verifiers (see Section 6) | Approve a variety form unless also a verifier for it |
| Verifier | Native speaker, assigned to one or more varieties | Approve or reject forms, examples, audio, and AI output **for their varieties** | Approve content for another variety; publish words |
| Admin | Staff | Create and edit words, manage tags and users, publish words after rules are met | Override a missing verifier approval |
| Learner | Registered user | Suggest corrections | Change live content |
| Guest | Anyone | Read published content | Anything else |

An admin who is also a verifier holds both permissions, and each action
is logged under the capacity used.

## 3. Who Verifies Words

### 3.1 Requirements for a verifier
- Native or heritage speaker of the variety, accepted by the language
  lead and recognized by at least one community organization or elder
- Signed verifier agreement (Section 8 of `permissions/`)
- Consent recorded on whether their name is shown beside forms
- **Minimum two verifiers per variety** where possible. If a variety has
  only one, every form that verifier approves is flagged
  "single-verifier" internally, and the risk is logged in the risk register.

### 3.2 Verification steps
1. A form is created by an admin or imported as `DRAFT`.
2. The admin submits it, and it becomes `NEEDS_REVIEW`.
3. A verifier for that variety checks spelling, romanization, meaning,
   and audio (if any), then approves or rejects with a reason.
4. Rejected forms return to `DRAFT` with the reason attached.
5. A word publishes only when **every** variety tag on it has an approved
   form. The publish check fails with a list of what is missing.

### 3.3 Sampling
For bulk imports, each verifier reviews every form in their variety up to
50 entries per batch. Above 50, they review a random 20% sample plus all
entries flagged by the validator. If any sample entry is rejected, the
whole batch for that variety returns for full review.

### 3.4 Single-verifier rule
Verifier cannot approve content they authored or imported themselves.
Another verifier approves it, or, where only one exists, it is
approved after a 48-hour waiting period and logged as self-approved.

## 4. Who Approves AI Content

| AI output | Approver | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Example sentence in a variety | Verifier for that variety | Must be fluent and natural, not just grammatical |
| Explanation or grammar note | Verifier for that variety; language lead for cross-variety notes | Must not state a rule the verifier cannot confirm |
| Quiz distractors | Admin | Must not be real words with a conflicting meaning |
| Translations of UI text | Admin | Not Magar content |

Rules:
1. AI output is stored as `NEEDS_REVIEW` with its model and prompt version.
2. AI **never** adds variety tags, marks a word as shared, or writes
   Magar forms for words that have no verified form.
3. Magar-language text generated by AI is labeled "AI-generated, reviewed
   by [verifier or 'a native speaker']" on the page.
4. Verifiers may edit before approving. The edited version is recorded as
   human-authored with AI assistance.
5. If a verifier rejects more than 30% of AI output in a month, the
   language lead reviews the prompts and may pause that AI feature.

## 5. Contributor Credit and Data Rights

- Each form records its source and, with consent, its contributor.
- Contributors may request correction, attribution changes, or removal.
- Removal requests: content is unpublished within **7 days** and deleted
  from the database and search indexes at the next index rebuild
  (maximum 30 days). Backups age out within 30 days.
- Audio recordings of a speaker are never used to train or fine-tune
  models, and are never shared with AI providers, unless the speaker
  gives separate written consent for that use.
- Data is not sold. Any commercial use needs written approval from the
  language lead and the affected community organizations.

## 6. Dispute Resolution

| Level | Situation | Who decides | Time limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Verifier pair | Two verifiers of the same variety disagree | They discuss and record the outcome. Where they cannot agree, **both forms are kept** as variants if both are in real use. | 7 days |
| 2. Language lead | Verifiers still disagree, or a learner or elder contests a published form | Language lead consults at least one additional community speaker, then decides. The decision and reasoning are logged. | 14 days |
| 3. Community panel | A dispute about a whole variety, naming, shared status, or a sensitive entry | Panel of one verifier per variety plus one community organization representative. Decision by consensus, recorded. | 30 days |

Rules during a dispute:
- The contested entry is set to `NEEDS_REVIEW` and hidden from the public
  if the disagreement is about correctness, or kept visible with a
  "contested" note if the disagreement is about preference.
- No one decides a dispute about their own contribution.
- Outcomes are written to the audit log, with a short public summary
  where the community agrees.
- A learner-reported error gets an acknowledgement within 3 days and an
  outcome within 14 days.

### 6.1 Conflict of interest
A person with a personal, family, or financial interest in an entry
declares it and steps out of the decision.

## 7. Withheld and Sensitive Content

- A verifier may mark any entry or whole topic as **withheld** at any time.
- Withheld entries are excluded from import, display, embeddings, audio
  generation, and AI prompts.
- The decision does not need to be explained publicly.
- A withheld entry is restored only on the same verifier's or the
  community panel's request.

| AI approval and rejection rates | Monthly | Language lead |

| This document | Every 6 months, or after any Level 3 dispute | Language lead and verifiers |

## 9. Turnaround Targets

| Task | Target |
|---|---|
| Verifier reviews a submitted form | 7 days |
| Admin publishes after approvals | 2 days |
| Learner-reported error: acknowledge / resolve | 3 / 14 days |
| Removal request: unpublish / full deletion | 7 / 30 days |
